- Maintain a neutral and objective tone. Present different perspectives fairly.
- Use visuals (images, diagrams) to illustrate key concepts and improve readability.
- Write at a 9th-10th grade reading level. Use tools like the Flesch-Kincaid readability test to assess the reading level of your writing.
- Define all medical terms in the glossary, and link to the glossary within the text.
- When discussing treatments, clearly explain what they do, how they work (in simple terms), and their potential benefits and side effects.
- Avoid making overly optimistic or pessimistic statements about the prognosis. Focus on providing realistic information about the disease course.
- Use headings and subheadings to organize the information logically.
- Use transition words and phrases to connect ideas and ensure smooth flow.
- Proofread carefully for grammar, spelling, and punctuation errors.
