# Research Plan for Writing a Wikipedia-Style Entry on Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy

## 1. Scope & Key Questions per Section
- **Lead / Short Summary**
  - What are the key facts about Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy (DMD)?
- **Overview / Definition**
  - How is DMD defined? What are its hallmark features?
- **Signs and Symptoms / Clinical Features**
  - What is the typical onset and progression of symptoms? What organ systems are affected?
- **Genetics & Pathophysiology**
  - What genes are involved? What is the inheritance pattern and penetrance?
- **Diagnosis**
  - What are the clinical criteria and recommended tests for diagnosis?
- **Management / Treatment**
  - What are the current standards of care and approved therapies?
- **Prognosis / Natural History**
  - What is the expected progression of the disease?
- **Epidemiology**
  - What is the prevalence of DMD? Are there geographic or demographic variations?
- **History / Nomenclature**
  - What is the historical context of DMD and its nomenclature?
- **Research & Ongoing Trials**
  - What are the notable ongoing studies and investigational therapies?

## 2. Search Strategy
- **PubMed Queries:**
  - "Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy review[Title/Abstract] AND (2013:2023[Date - Publication])"
  - "Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy genetics[Title/Abstract] AND (2013:2023[Date - Publication])"
  - "Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy treatment guidelines[Title/Abstract] AND (2013:2023[Date - Publication])"
- **Orphanet/GARD/GeneReviews:**
  - Search for "Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy" on each platform.
- **ClinicalTrials.gov:**
  - Filter for "Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy" and "Active" studies.
- **Relevant Society Guidelines:**
  - Search for guidelines from ACMG, AAN, and other relevant organizations.

## 3. Screening Rules
- Include only sources published in the last 10 years.
- Prefer meta-analyses and consensus guidelines.
- Include cohort studies with sample sizes (N≥10).
- Case reports only if they describe ultra-rare variants (mark as low-N).

## 4. Evidence Grading
- **Guidelines** > **Reviews** > **Cohorts** > **Case Series** > **Case Reports**

## 5. Extraction Schema
- **Inheritance:** X-linked recessive
- **Gene:** DMD gene
- **Prevalence:** ~1 in 3,500 male births
- **Hallmark Signs:** Progressive muscle weakness, Gowers' sign
- **Differentials:** Becker Muscular Dystrophy, Spinal Muscular Atrophy
- **Diagnostic Tests:** Creatine kinase levels, genetic testing, muscle biopsy
- **Treatments:** Corticosteroids, gene therapy, supportive care
- **Monitoring:** Cardiac and respiratory function assessments
- **Outcomes:** Life expectancy, quality of life measures

## 6. Tables/Figures Planned
- **Genotype–Phenotype Table:** Overview of DMD mutations and associated clinical features.
- **Differential Diagnosis Table:** Key differentiators between DMD and similar conditions.
- **Therapy Summary Table:** Overview of current treatments and their indications.

## 7. Risk & Bias Checks
- Assess for outdated prevalence data and small-N bias in studies.
- Note potential founder effects and ascertainment bias in epidemiological data.
- Identify and document any controversies in treatment approaches or genetic findings.

## 8. Milestones & Outputs with Timestamps
- **M1: Sources Assembled (Week 1)**
  - Complete literature search and compile a list of relevant articles and guidelines.
- **M2: Extraction Complete (Week 2)**
  - Extract key information from the selected sources into the schema.
- **M3: Draft (Week 3)**
  - Write the first draft of the Wikipedia-style entry.
- **M4: Fact-Check Pass (Week 4)**
  - Review citations for accuracy and ensure neutrality; finalize the entry.
